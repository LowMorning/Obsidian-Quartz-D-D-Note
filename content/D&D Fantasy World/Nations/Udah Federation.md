A nation located upon the continent of [[Ulavia]].

**Udah Federation** is a Federation of all Races.  The de facto successor of the [[Udah Empire]], an nation once span the entirety of [[Ulavia]].  After the [[Imperial Schism]], the [[Udah Empire]] were split.  **Udah Federation** being the name sake successor.

At one point everyone under [[Ulavia]] would call themselves an [[Udan]].  Though these days, only those from the Federation would say so.

It can be said that the federation is more of a loose alliance than a single state. After the fall of the [[Udah Empire]], the governors of these states joined together and form the foundation of the **Udah Federation**.

The federation is make up of  7 [[Ministered Provinces]].  2 [[Northern Provinces]], 2 [[Eastern Provinces]], 2 [[Western Provinces]], and finally the [[Capital Province]].
