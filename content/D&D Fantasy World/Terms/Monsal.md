>"It is said in legends that Monsals are mirrors, reflections, of sentient beings.  That for every living being, there exists its match of Monsal.  For every great hero, a calamity exist.  I found this to be laughable, for I have yet to see a Monsal who doesn't want me dead despite the villains that gathered at any Capital"
>						*- Of the World and Beings Volume 1* Professor [[Hoppa Lingoberry]]

Monsals have exists since [[Beings of Higher Power]] created the world.  No one really know exactly how they comes to be and many legends are written about their origins.  Some theorized they are the creation of a Demonic [[Beings of Higher Power|God]], some say they are sinner hit by divine punishments delivered by [[Beings of Higher Power|Gods]] when they left the mortal plane.

Whatever they are, they are an exact replica of any sentient species that exists.  Same look, build, natural gift, and perhaps even sound if they would speak.  For one reason or another, despite some are even gifted enough to cast spells, they cannot communicate and lack any ability to form a sociality.  In a way, they lack the sentiency that separate a ordinary man from beasts.

One mystery that no one has solved is how they are born.  Monsals would seemingly appear where ever civilization is missing, and even if an area is clear of them they would slowly populate the area again despite no sighting of new Monsals migrating in the area.
