An Frontier village within the [[Udah Federation]].  Bordering the [[Lostsian Theocracy]] and the [[Unclaimed Wilderness]].

Located within the [[Barony of Ulaster]] under Baron [[Plad Redos]].  It is about a 6 hours walk from [[Acomb]] and 9 hours walk from the Baron Capital of [[Ulaster]].

Currently under control of Steward [[Harris Bender]], given authority by the Baron.  

The [[Misfits]] has taken a quest to exterminate an report of an [[Monsal]] goblin camp.  Discovered it was in fact not [[Monsal]] and also saved [[David Bender]], the son of the Steward.
