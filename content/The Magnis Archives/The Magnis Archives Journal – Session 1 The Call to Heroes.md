Praise be to [[Yondalla]] on this day for she has guided me to [[Biamont]], a dusty crossroads in the borderlands. Steward [[Harris Bender]], a half-orc with more scar than smile, was offering gold for heroes to fight rampaging goblin [[Monsal|Monsals]] growing  bolder, cleverer, organized. I feel this has been ripped from one of [[Hoppa Lingoberry|Professor Lingoberry]]’s tales of frontier perils - life imitates art!

Before I could finish my first drink, [[Yondalla]] saw fit to surround me with company: [[Bonnie]], a fairy brighter than dawn and twice as chaotic; [[Keranous Au Raa]], a paladin so tall I get a nosebleed looking up; [[Alice Grace]], a proud forest elf with her wolf Miki, with fangs sharper than daggers; and [[Dimsum Hagao]], an Owlin barbarian enchanted with “shinies”. 

The steward offered a thousand gold. [[Dimsum Hagao|Hagao]] haggled another five hundred — truly a holy number. [[Keranous Au Raa|Keranous]] pledged to bring back goblin heads and demanded a metal statue in his honor. Only [[Yondalla]] knows where [[Biamont]] could find iron nails let alone metal for a statue…

As we poked around the tavern the dwarven barkeep warned me of scheming nobles called [[The Lions]] — the plotting sort. [[Keranous Au Raa|Keranous]] confronted a nervous hooded man, Bolder Hun, trying (poorly) to eavesdrop. From him we learned of a raided caravan three hours southeast. Seems our path was already laid out.

The road was calm until [[Alice Grace|Alice]]’s ears twitched - goblins in the brush. [[Dimsum Hagao|Hagao]] launched himself from a tree like feathery artillery and missed everything. [[Bonnie]] charmed one goblin into murdering his friend, I encouraged another to reconsider his career choices, and [[Keranous Au Raa|Keranous]], [[Alice Grace|Alice]], and Miki finished the rest. [[Dimsum Hagao|Hagao]] later redeemed himself with a magnificent two-for-one cleave. On their bodies we found strange runes and a map showing a hidden entrance to their camp.

The caravan itself was wrecked. Wagons torn apart, strongboxes empty. Among the debris we found a locked metal box and a letter from [[Ingvar Ironfist]], Marshal to the Baron, addressed to [[Harris Bender|Steward Bender]]. It detailed the potion shipment’s route — meaning someone knew exactly when to strike. Goblins don’t read ledgers, so someone else is pulling strings.

Guided once again by [[Yondalla]]’s unseen hand, we reached the goblin camp and took a cheeky short rest before the storm. The secret entrance ran along a rippling stream — perfect for sneaking in damp and unseen. I sent my bat familiar, Reginald, ahead. Inside he spotted well-armed hobgoblins, a goblin chieftain, and more little green headaches.

We worked smart: illusions drew off the edges, and we ambushed them one by one. [[Dimsum Hagao|Hagao]] took off a hobgoblin’s head in a single swing. Then the main assault began — [[Keranous Au Raa|Keranous]] charged, [[Bonnie]] webbed half the cave, [[Dimsum Hagao|Hagao]] bellowed a challenge to the chief, [[Alice Grace|Alice]] called down silver fire while Miki tore through the ranks, and [[Reginald]] even got a few heroic nips in. As for me — I loosed a perfectly timed magic missile that may have scuffed a hobgoblin’s armor. I’ll take the win.

When the dust settled, we found [[David Bender]], the steward’s son — alive, ungrateful, and loud. A gentle cantrip calmed his nerves. More importantly, we found treasure. [[Yondalla]]’s cornucopia overfloweth!
